Leaf Dash privacy policy

Applies to the Leaf Dash Android app and the Leaf Dash website. Last updated 27 November 2026.

In short

Leaf Dash has no accounts, no analytics, no adverts and no crash reports, and its developer runs no servers. Everything it reads from your car stays on your phone, unless you switch on an add-on that sends it somewhere you choose. The car's VIN is never sent anywhere.

What stays on your phone

Car readings, trip and charging history, battery health readings, settings, saved map areas, drive recordings and health trip reports are stored only on your phone, in the app's own storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them. You can save a backup file yourself (Settings → Your data); where it goes is up to you.

Those services have their own privacy policies. The map servers see your phone's internet address, as any website does.

Permissions the Android app asks for

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): to find and connect to your OBD adapter. It isn't used to work out where you are.
  • Location: so the Drive screen can follow the road you're on. It's asked for the first time the Drive screen needs it.
  • Internet: for map data and the add-ons above.

Your phone's own services

Android provides the GPS position, and spoken updates use the phone's text-to-speech engine. These are covered by your phone's own privacy settings.

Children

Leaf Dash is a tool for drivers and isn't aimed at children.
