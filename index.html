Drive recorder · read-only
Off

Records everything the app sees on a drive into one file you can share: car values, adapter replies, GPS, the map data and what the road drawing matched. Start it before you set off, keep Leaf Dash on screen, and stop it when you park. The VIN is never recorded, but the file does contain your route. The last three recordings are kept here.

Real road shape is off, so no map data will be recorded.

Health trip recording · read-only
Off

Turn this on, then drive. It starts when you pull away and stops after 3 minutes parked in P (or when you disconnect). Cells are read every 2 s during the trip, and at the end you get a battery health report plus a raw log you can share. Reports are kept below, so you can reopen one you missed.

Mode scanner · read-only
2018+ cars

Finds which of the car's values follow a light, pedal or button. Each test turns one thing on and back off, and the values that follow it are listed. Read-only requests only.

CAN sniffer · listen only
Stopped

Silent monitor (ATCSM1): the adapter only listens and never transmits or acknowledges. Battery polling pauses while it runs. Bytes that just changed light up. Tap a row to see its bits.

Of limited use on a 2018+ Leaf: the car's gateway only passes request/reply traffic to the OBD port, so there's little to hear. Some adapters, including the LELink², can't listen silently at all, and the sniffer then stays off. The mode scanner above is the way to find values on these cars.
